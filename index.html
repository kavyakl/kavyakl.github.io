Research

I build ML systems that make neural networks smaller, faster, and more energy-efficient on constrained hardware. My work connects algorithmic compression (pruning, sparsity) with compiler-level transformations and real deployment on microcontrollers and edge devices.

Activation-Aware MLP Compression

End-to-end pipeline for pruning MLPs with ReLU, Sigmoid, and Tanh using activation profiling, correlation-regression analysis, and activation-specific thresholds. Validated on MCUs (RP2040, ESP32) with measured speed, memory, and energy benefits.

CNN Compression and Dynamic Sparse Training

Structured pruning and dynamic sparse training for CNNs (LeNet-5, VGG-11), integrated with quantization and ONNX export. Focuses on deployment-ready sparsity patterns that map efficiently to edge and GPU runtimes.

Compiler-Aware Inference and Edge Deployment

ONNX-based model transformations, quantization-aware pipelines, and hardware-cost modeling that translate algorithmic sparsity into real latency, memory, and energy improvements on microcontrollers, GPUs, and FPGAs.

The common thread is systems: turning compression ideas into measurable gains on real hardware, not just simulated FLOPs.
